# Visualization Literacy Study

## Welcome!

Hi, I'm Anna Kurtenkova, and I'm a student at the University of Vienna conducting this study as part of my Master's thesis. Thank you for taking the time to help out.

This study looks at how people read and understand different kinds of charts. No special background is needed.
&NewLine


#### What will you be doing?

You'll answer a few questions about yourself and how you feel about reading charts, go through a short visualization literacy test, and then look at a series of charts and answer questions about them. All questions just involve clicking or picking an answer.

**Duration:** About 30 minutes.



#### Before you start, a few practical things:

* Please complete this on a **laptop or desktop computer**, parts of the study involve clicking precise spots on a chart, which won't work correctly on a phone or tablet.
* Please try to **complete the whole thing in one sitting**, somewhere quiet where you can focus.
* Please **answer based on your own judgment**, without looking anything up or asking someone else for help. We're interested in how **you** read these charts. This isn't just a formality because we are measuring how your responses across the three parts relate to each other, any outside help or interruption would distort that relationship and make your individual results less meaningful, which affects the research as a whole.

#### Data is anonymous: 

We do not collect your name, email, or any other information that could identify you.
