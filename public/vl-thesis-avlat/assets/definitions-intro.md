## Terminology 

This study uses two specific terms in the next part, they are explained below:

### Median

If you lined up every data point in a dataset from smallest to largest, the median is the one sitting exactly in the middle. Half the data points are smaller than the median, and half are larger.

<img src="vl-thesis-avlat/assets/charts/median_example.png" width="50%">

### Outlier

An outlier is a data point that clearly stands apart from the rest of the data. 

**Tip:** To decide whether a point counts as an outlier, we use a simple rule: look at the middle 50% of the data, measure how wide that middle chunk is, and multiply that width by 1.5. Any point sitting further than that distance from the middle chunk counts as an outlier.
