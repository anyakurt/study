## Visualization Literacy Test

This is the **first part of the study**. You'll see a series of charts, each with a short question and a few answer choices — pick the one you think is correct.

This will take about **10 minutes**.

Answer based on your own knowledge. Every question also has a **"Skip" option** - if you genuinely don't know the answer, please choose "Skip" rather than guessing. A lucky guess would count as correct even though you didn't actually know it, which makes our data less accurate. This isn't a test you need to score well on, so there's no reason to try to guess your way to a better result :)
